Getting started

Three steps to your first send.

You’ll need a supported Windows PC, a compatible GSM modem, and a SIM card with SMS service.

01

Install the app

Download the installer that matches your Windows system and follow its prompts.

02

Connect your modem

Attach your GSM modem and make sure its SIM card is active and ready to send SMS.

03

Load a list and send

Import your CSV or Excel contact list, compose your message, and review the results.

System requirements

Windows 10 or later (64-bit), or Windows 8/8.1 (32-bit) with the matching installer. A compatible GSM modem and active SIM are required to send SMS.
